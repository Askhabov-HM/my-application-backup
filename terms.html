About using the utility

Terms of use

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Purpose and operator

My Application Backup is a personal utility operated by Askhabov-HM. It creates encrypted application backups and stores them in the account owner's Google Drive. This website describes the utility and its data practices.

Authorized use

The utility is used with the account owner's authorization. The owner selects the application data to back up and is responsible for having permission to copy and store that data. This website does not offer public account registration.

Owner controls

The owner manages the backup schedule, retention settings, host access and encryption keys. The owner can stop backup operations and revoke the Google Drive connection at any time.

Availability and recovery

Operation depends on the owner's host and Google Drive. Backup and restore checks help detect failures, but successful recovery also requires access to the stored backup and its encryption key. The utility is provided on an as-available basis without a service-level commitment.

Privacy

Handling of application data, Google Drive files and authorization information is described in the Privacy policy.

Contact and changes

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub issues. Do not post credentials or private backup data in public issues. Updates to these terms will be published here with a revised effective date.
