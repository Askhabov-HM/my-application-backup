Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

My Application Backup is a personal backup utility operated by Askhabov-HM. This policy explains how the utility handles application backups, Google Drive data and authorization information.

Information the utility handles

  • Application database snapshots selected by the owner for backup.
  • Google Drive backup files and the metadata needed to manage them, such as file identifiers, names, sizes and modification times.
  • OAuth authorization information, including access and refresh tokens, needed to keep the authorized Google Drive connection working.
  • Operational records needed to diagnose backup and restore failures.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The connection is used only to store and manage backups: uploading encrypted files, locating and listing backup objects, downloading them for verification or recovery, and removing expired backup objects under the owner's retention settings.

The requested scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. Access is limited to files created by this application or explicitly made available to it. The utility does not request access to Gmail, contacts or calendars.

Storage and protection

Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's host before upload. Encrypted backup objects are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Encryption keys and authorization information remain in private configuration under the operator's control and are not included in this public website or its repository.

Local snapshots, working files and operational records may be retained on the owner's host as required for backup and recovery. The operator controls access to that host and to the encryption keys.

Sharing and use of Google data

Google Drive receives encrypted backup objects and the associated file metadata needed to store them. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. The utility does not otherwise share Google user data with third parties.

My Application Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Backups are retained according to the settings chosen by the owner. The owner can delete backup files from Google Drive and remove local copies, operational records and connection credentials from the host.

The owner can revoke the application's access through the Google Account settings for third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; existing backup files remain until the owner deletes them.

This information website

These static pages do not use application cookies, analytics scripts or forms to collect credentials or backup contents. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator through the project's GitHub issues. Public issues are visible to other people, so do not include credentials, backup contents or other private information.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
